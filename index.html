The Brand

Leather made with intention.

Jabey Leather Craft creates handcrafted leather goods with a focus on simplicity, craftsmanship and individuality. Each piece is made carefully by hand and designed for everyday use.

The Craft

Made by hand. Made to last.

Every piece begins with carefully selected leather and is shaped, stitched and finished by hand. The process is deliberately slow, allowing attention to be given to every detail.

From everyday wallets to personalised pieces, our goal is simple: create leather goods that become part of your everyday life.
